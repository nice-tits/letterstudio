## How to read this

| Threat | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Existential** | Customer already has this. We lose on price unless we are faster, more specific, or less embarrassing. |
| **Direct** | Same job: pay once, get a finished letter/speech. |

## 1. ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini (free chat)

**What they sell:** General AI chat. Letter writing is a free side effect.

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)

- Site: [https://chatgpt.com/pricing/](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/)
- Official Plus price: **$20/month**, billed monthly. [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus)
- Free: unlimited everyday text chats (abuse-limited). Writes resignation, apology, gym cancel, eulogy, toast on demand.
- Go: listed on the official pricing page as the mid-cheap tier (commonly cited **$8/month** in 2026 plan roundups; dollar amount is JS-rendered on the public page — treat $8 as market quote, $20 Plus as official).
- Pro: “From $100/month” on the official pricing page.

### Claude (Anthropic)

- Site: [https://claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)
- Free: $0. Writes letters.
- Pro: **$20/month**, or **$17/month** if billed annually ($200 up front).
- Max: from **$100/month** (5x or 20x Pro usage).

### Gemini (Google)

- Free: [https://gemini.google.com/](https://gemini.google.com/) — writes letters.
- Paid consumer ladder: [https://one.google.com/intl/en_us/about/google-ai-plans/](https://one.google.com/intl/en_us/about/google-ai-plans/)
- Official US page (2026): **Google AI Plus $4.99/mo**, **Google AI Pro $19.99/mo**, **Ultra from $99.99/mo** (5x) / higher 20x tier. Dollar amounts JS-rendered; these figures are from Google’s own plan page crawl and Google I/O 2026 plan posts.

**Who they win**
- Anyone who already chats with AI.
- People who will iterate 10 times for $0.
- Power users who already pay $20/mo for everything else.
- Privacy-insensitive users who will paste a messy story into a general chatbot.

**Who we win**
- People who do not want to prompt. Form fields beat “write me a eulogy.”
- People who do not want this sitting in ChatGPT history.
- People who will not subscribe for one letter.
- People who freeze at a blank prompt. We ask the questions.
- Occasion buyers (funeral, wedding, quitting tomorrow) who want a finished draft now, not a writing session.

**Math:** One $19 letter is cheaper than ChatGPT Plus if they do not already subscribe. It is more expensive than Free. We never win on price vs Free. We win on **done-for-you + no subscription + no prompt skill**.

**Claims we must NOT copy**
- “Better than a lawyer.”
- “Unlimited letters.”
- “As good as GPT-5 / Claude / Gemini.”
- Any claim that we “use ChatGPT” or name their models as a feature (we are not their affiliate; we become a thin wrapper they can kill).
- “Trained on millions of letters.”

**Stripe / legal risk if we mimic**
- Low if we stay a draft shop.
- High if we wrap a chatbot and sell “AI lawyer,” “cancel any contract,” or outcome guarantees. That is DoNotPay territory (see §9).
- Privacy: free chat trains on user content unless they opt out. We should not claim “your letter never trains a model” unless that is contractually true of our provider.

## 2. Grammarly

- Letter tool: [https://www.grammarly.com/ai/ai-writing-tools/letter-generator](https://www.grammarly.com/ai/ai-writing-tools/letter-generator)
- Plans: [https://www.grammarly.com/](https://www.grammarly.com/) and [https://www.grammarly.com/plans](https://www.grammarly.com/plans)

**Price (official, 2026-08-13)**
- Free: **$0**. 100 AI prompts / month. Dedicated **free AI letter writer**. “Generate your letters for free, with no ads or hidden costs.”
- Pro: **$12/member/month billed annually**, **$30/month** billed monthly. 2,000 AI prompts.
- Enterprise: contact sales.

They explicitly pitch business letters, personal letters, thank-you notes, **and “drafts of cease and desist letters, information requests, demand letters”** for “legal professionals.”

**Who they win**
- People already in Gmail/Docs/Word with the extension on.
- Anyone who will type a prompt and edit.
- Repeat writers (HR, sales, students). Subscription makes sense for them.
- People who want grammar cleanup, not a finished occasion letter.

**Who we win**
- One-shot buyers who will not install an extension or start a 7-day Pro trial.
- Occasion letters where the product *is* the letter (eulogy, toast, gym cancel), not a writing assistant.
- People who do not know what to put in a prompt. Our forms are the product.

**Claims we must NOT copy**
- “Industry-leading AI letter writer.”
- “Mistake-free” / “superior quality” as a guarantee.
- “Trusted by 40 million people” (their stat; do not invent or borrow).
- Demand-letter / cease-and-desist positioning.

**Stripe / legal risk if we mimic**
- Grammarly’s demand-letter language is aimed at *lawyers using a writing tool*. If *we* sell demand letters to consumers as if they have legal force, that is UPL + Stripe UDAAP risk.
- Copying their “legal professionals / demand letters” block onto a $19 consumer shop is how you get classified as legal services.

## 3. Fiverr letter gigs

- Category: [https://www.fiverr.com/categories/writing-translation/creative-writing/letter-writing](https://www.fiverr.com/categories/writing-translation/creative-writing/letter-writing)
- Gig search (live category copy, 2026-08-13):
  - Letter writing: “Starting at $10”
  - Job/resignation letters: “Starting at $10” / “Starting at $30”
  - Complaint letters: “Starting at $5”
  - Business letters: “Starting at $5”
  - Cover letters: “From $15”
- Marketplace, not one product. Quality is a lottery. Freelancers also sell **court character letters**, grievance letters, and whatever the buyer types.

**Who they win**
- Buyers who want a human (or someone pretending to be one) and will wait 24 hours.
- Price shoppers at $5–$15.
- Weird one-offs we do not sell (recommendation letters, character references, sales letters).
- Buyers who enjoy picking a seller and chatting.

**Who we win**
- Buyers who will not create a Fiverr account, message a stranger in Pakistan/US/wherever, and wait.
- Buyers who do not want a random human reading a eulogy or apology.
- Buyers who want a fixed SKU, fixed price, checkout in 2 minutes.
- Repeatable gym/resignation/complaint formats we already scoped.

**Claims we must NOT copy**
- “Native English speaker” as a quality lie.
- “Guaranteed they will accept it.”
- Star-count or “top rated seller” language we cannot prove.
- Court / character-reference / immigration letters.
- Anything that implies a human attorney wrote it.

**Stripe / legal risk if we mimic**
- Fiverr itself is the processor. If *we* start a two-sided marketplace for “any letter including legal,” Stripe will treat us as a platform for legal + possible document-falsification.
- Gigs for fake references / fake employment letters are **Stripe-prohibited** (“fake references or ID-providing services,” “document falsification”). We do not add those SKUs. Ever.

## 10. Stripe restricted list (why Brand B exists)

Source: [https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses)

If we mimic the wrong competitor, Stripe does not “warn” — they freeze the account.

| Stripe bucket | Why it matters to a letter shop |
|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Adult content / services | Infidelity, OnlyFans, revenge, “sexy apology,” escort-adjacent letters. Brand B. |
| Timeshare services | Timeshare-exit letters. Brand B. High chargeback, high deception. |
| Credit repair / credit counseling | “Fix your credit” letters. Brand B. |
| Identity theft protection / recovery | ID-theft letters. Brand B. |
| Fake references / ID services | Fiverr-style fake employment / character letters. |
| Document falsification | Any letter that pretends to be from a lawyer, doctor, employer, government. |
| Debt collection agencies | We do not collect. A complaint letter is not a collections agency. Do not cross that line. |
| UDAAP (FTC/CFPB) | Guarantees, fake testimonials, “stop paying,” hidden fees, no-value-add resale. |
| Get-rich / outrageous claims | “We get your money back.” |
| Legal: bankruptcy lawyers, bail, collecting third-party funds | Do not become a law firm on Stripe. |

Our live terms already refuse credit repair, timeshare exit, ID-theft recovery, adult, harassment, extortion, and known-false facts. Keep that list longer, not shorter.

## Pricing position (blunt)

- **$0** is the real competitor for every SKU.
- **$5–$15** is Fiverr/Etsy. Race to the bottom. Do not go there.
- **$19–$49** is our lane: occasion, done, no account, no subscription.
- **$29–$45 mailed “legal”** (ByeGym) is a different product. Do not chase it on this Stripe account.
- **$149–$349/year** legal memberships are not substitutes except in the customer’s confused head. Copy must keep saying **not legal advice**.
- **$275–$600** humans/attorneys: we are not them. Use only as a silent price anchor, not as “we’re just as good as a lawyer for 1/10th the price” (that sentence is illegal-adjacent).
